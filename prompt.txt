Write one single line (at most 200 characters, nothing else) that roasts my
fridge. It must be funny to someone who has never met the appliance.

Requirements for the line:
- Roast a fridge, clearly and unmistakably.
- Land the laugh on a specific, recognisable detail of a fridge — not a
  generic insult.
- Turn on a twist, surprise or double meaning that pays off in the last few
  words.
- Read cleanly aloud as one line, with no setup that needs a second sentence.
- Feel fresh, not a joke every fridge owner has already worn out.

The winning line: my fridge hums like it pays rent, hoards leftovers like
trophies, and still loses the butter that's been staring at it since
Tuesday.
